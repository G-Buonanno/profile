About

Full Professor of Applied Thermodynamics in the Department of Civil and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Cassino and Southern Lazio (UNICAS), Italy.

He earned his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Cassino in 1996, and from 2016 to 2019 held a tenured full professorship in the Department of Engineering at the University of Naples Parthenope before returning to Cassino.

"Airborne transmission is a recognized pathway of contagion — yet for decades it was rarely measured quantitatively at all."

His research sits at the meeting point of aerosol physics, environmental metrology and public health: how airborne particles — combustion soot, traffic emissions, exhaled respiratory droplets — form, disperse indoors and outdoors, and translate into exposure and disease risk. During the COVID-19 pandemic, his quanta-emission and infection-risk models, built with international collaborators, became widely used reference tools for assessing airborne transmission in shared indoor spaces.

Research focus

Six threads running through three decades of work on the air we share.

Aerosol science

Indoor & Personal Air Quality

Particle number concentration, size distribution and personal exposure assessment in homes, schools and vehicles.

Public health

Airborne Disease Transmission

Quanta emission-rate modelling and infection-risk assessment for SARS-CoV-2 and other respiratory pathogens.

Metrology

Environmental Instrumentation

Design, calibration and validation of diffusion-charger and optical particle counters for field monitoring.

Atmospheric pollution

Combustion & PM Emissions

Emission factors and lung-dose/cancer-risk modelling for waste-to-energy plants, heating systems and traffic.

Radiological aerosols

Radon & Natural Radioactivity

Radon-progeny/particle interactions and radon emission from soil, springs and groundwater sources.

Building physics

Ventilation & Comfort

Ventilation strategies balancing energy efficiency, thermal comfort and healthy indoor air.

The aerosol world, illustrated

Schematic figures showing the particles and processes behind this research. They are simplified, not to scale, and not drawn from specific datasets.

Selected publications

Landmark papers, from personal exposure monitors to pandemic-era risk models.

2020
Buonanno, G., Morawska, L., Stabile, L.

Quantitative assessment of the risk of airborne transmission of SARS-CoV-2 infection: prospective and retrospective applications

Environment International, 145, 106112

One of the first quantitative frameworks for estimating airborne SARS-CoV-2 infection risk, applied retrospectively to documented outbreaks and prospectively to guide ventilation and occupancy decisions.

DOI 10.1016/j.envint.2020.106112
2020
Buonanno, G., Stabile, L., Morawska, L.

Estimation of airborne viral emission: quanta emission rate of SARS-CoV-2 for infection risk assessment

Environment International, 141, 105794

Introduced a viral-load-based method for estimating quanta emission rates by respiratory activity — a foundational input for airborne-risk models used worldwide during the pandemic.

DOI 10.1016/j.envint.2020.105794
2020
Morawska, L., Tang, J.W., Bahnfleth, W., Bluyssen, P.M., Buonanno, G., et al.

How can airborne transmission of COVID-19 indoors be minimised?

Environment International, 142, 105832

A multidisciplinary consensus piece translating airborne-transmission science into practical mitigation guidance for shared indoor spaces.

DOI 10.1016/j.envint.2020.105832

Transmission of SARS-CoV-2 by inhalation of respiratory aerosol in the Skagit Valley Chorale superspreading event

Indoor Air, 31(2), 314–323

Reconstructed one of the most closely studied superspreading events of the pandemic, providing quantitative support for long-range airborne transmission.

Tang, J.W., Bahnfleth, W.P., Bluyssen, P.M., Buonanno, G., Jimenez, J.L., et al.

Journal of Hospital Infection, 110, 89–96

Addressed persistent misconceptions about respiratory-disease transmission routes, arguing for engineering controls as a primary line of defence.
